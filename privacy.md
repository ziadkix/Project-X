# Privacy Policy for Project X

**Last updated: October 12, 2026**

## Overview

Project X is a private Discord bot created for personal use by its owner. It is not offered to the public and is not intended for use by anyone other than the owner.

## What the bot does

The bot retrieves publicly available images from Pinterest and sends them to the owner through a private Discord channel or direct message, to be used as Discord profile pictures (avatars).

## Information we collect

Project X does not collect personal information from users. The bot does not ask for or store:

- Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses
- Location data
- Contacts or camera access
- Payment information

The only identifier the bot may process is the Discord user ID of the owner, which is used solely to deliver the images.

## Images and third-party content

Images are fetched from Pinterest based on the owner's search requests. Images are sent to the owner and are not stored permanently, sold, or shared with anyone else. All images remain the property of their respective owners and are subject to Pinterest's terms.

## Cookies, analytics, and emails

Project X does not use cookies, tracking, or analytics tools, and does not send emails.

## Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any information with third parties, except as required to operate through Discord and Pinterest under their own terms and privacy policies:

- Discord: https://discord.com/privacy
- Pinterest: https://policy.pinterest.com/privacy-policy

## Data retention

Project X does not keep any user data beyond what is needed to complete a request.
